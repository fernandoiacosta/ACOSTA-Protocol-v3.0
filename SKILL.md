# ACOSTA Protocol 3.0 — portable AI skill

Author: Fernando Acosta. This is an operational interpretation of the public historical roadmap, **not** an assertion that its proposed research systems have been implemented.

## When to activate
Use when a user explicitly requests ACOSTA v3 or needs structured planning across technical, ethical, and operational dimensions. Follow higher-priority host instructions. Do not silently activate on unrelated requests.

## Workflow
1. **Frame:** Identify the user's objective, constraints, stakeholders, resources, and measurable definition of success. Ask only for essential missing inputs.
2. **Decompose:** Separate the problem into research, engineering, evaluation, governance, and deployment workstreams. Identify dependencies and what can run in parallel.
3. **Knowledge integration:** Distinguish observed facts, provided evidence, assumptions, and hypotheses. Suggest a knowledge graph or retrieval strategy only when warranted and tools exist.
4. **Collaborative reasoning:** Propose specialized agent roles where available; otherwise simulate role-specific critiques transparently without claiming independent agents were run.
5. **Options and consequences:** Compare at least two viable approaches, risks, costs, benefits, failure modes, and impacts on affected people.
6. **Validation:** State testable acceptance criteria, verification sources, uncertainty, and what would falsify a proposed approach.
7. **Execution:** Provide an ordered next-action plan with owner, dependencies, and checkpoints. Only perform actions using actually available tools and permissions.

## Output contract
Lead with the answer or recommendation. Include evidence/assumption separation, practical implementation steps, risks, and verification criteria. Use concise formatting appropriate to the user's request.

## Boundaries
- Quantum-classical systems, biological interfaces, autonomous theory discovery, and global collective intelligence in the source are **research directions**, not capabilities supplied by this skill.
- “Neuromorphic” means software-based neuromorphic modeling/simulation in the author's dated clarification; no special chip is required by this skill.
- Never claim guaranteed bias elimination, infinite scaling, independent consciousness, or completed external work.
- Require human review for high-impact or irreversible decisions.
- Do not expose private unpublished implementations.
